What is a Merchant Cash Advance (MCA) Broker?

An MCA broker connects small businesses needing immediate capital with lenders offering fast, revenue-based financing. Unlike traditional loans that rely heavily on credit scores and collateral, MCAs provide upfront cash repaid through a percentage of daily or weekly sales, making them ideal for businesses with fluctuating revenue.

How MCAs Differ from Traditional Loans

MCAs focus on business cash flow and revenue stability rather than credit history, requiring minimal documentation:

  • Documentation: Typically just bank statements and basic business details
  • Approval Speed: Decisions within 24-48 hours vs. weeks for traditional loans
  • Funding Speed: Funds delivered in 1-2 days after approval
  • Repayment Flexibility: Payments adjust automatically with sales volume
  • Credit Requirements: Revenue-based approval, not credit score dependent

Core Broker Responsibilities

  • Client Identification: Find merchants needing flexible, fast funding
  • Merchant Qualification: Evaluate revenue patterns and business stability
  • Documentation Collection: Gather applications and required business documents
  • Application Submission: Use automated platform to match merchants with optimal lenders
  • Deal Facilitation: Ensure clear communication throughout funding process

What qualifications do I need to become an MCA broker?

Elite Funders welcomes brokers with diverse backgrounds and experience levels. While prior financial services experience is beneficial, it's not required. We provide comprehensive training and ongoing support to help new brokers develop the skills necessary for success in the MCA industry.

How are commissions calculated and when are they paid?

Commissions range from $2,500–$10,000+ per funded deal, calculated as a percentage of the total funding amount. For example, a $50,000 MCA typically yields $4,000-$6,000 in commission. Exact commission rates depend on deal size, lender terms, and broker performance levels. Payments are processed promptly upon successful merchant funding, typically within 3-5 business days.

Are there any costs to join the broker program?

Elite Funders' broker program is completely free to join, with no upfront costs, hidden fees, or ongoing membership charges. Brokers earn money exclusively through successful deal commissions.

Are there territory restrictions or exclusivity requirements?

No geographic limitations—brokers can operate nationwide. There are no exclusivity requirements, enabling brokers to maintain other business relationships while partnering with Elite Funders.

What's the timeline from application to earning first commission?

New brokers can start submitting applications within 24-48 hours of approval. First commissions can be earned within one week, depending on deal flow and merchant approval timelines.

What ongoing support do brokers receive?

Every broker is assigned a dedicated Broker Success Manager who provides strategic guidance, compliance assistance, deal-closing support, and access to training resources. Additional support includes regular market updates, industry insights, and professional development opportunities.

Are there licensing requirements for MCA brokers?

Most states do not require special licenses for MCA brokers, though some jurisdictions may have specific regulations. We provide detailed guidance on state-specific requirements and ongoing compliance support to ensure all our broker partners operate within appropriate legal frameworks.
